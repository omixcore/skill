# AI 辅助生成文件与内容交付前检查规范 / AI-Assisted Artifact Pre-delivery Quality-Control Standard

本规范适用于所有使用 AI 辅助创建、编辑、转换或整理的交付物，包括文字、论文、图片、插图、图表、SVG、PDF、Word、PowerPoint、Excel、代码、数据文件及压缩包。核心目标是清理意外残留的生成过程痕迹和无关信息，保证文件整洁、完整、可编辑、可复现；同时不得破坏真实性、版权或来源证明。

This standard applies to all deliverables created, edited, converted, or organized with AI assistance, including text, manuscripts, images, illustrations, charts, SVG, PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, code, data files, and archives. Its purpose is to remove accidental generation artifacts and irrelevant information while ensuring that files remain clean, complete, editable, and reproducible—without compromising authenticity, copyright, or provenance.

## 中文版

### 1. 总体要求

- 所有正式交付前，必须检查文件本身和文件内容，不得只检查可见页面。
- 检查范围包括文件名、可见文字、图像内容、页眉页脚、图注、备注、批注、隐藏对象、图层、文档属性、元数据、嵌入资源及源代码注释。
- 清理意外出现的工具提示词、对话残留、临时标记、调试信息、默认占位符、无关品牌宣传和不属于最终内容的生成过程说明。
- 不得把清理工作理解为伪造作者、伪造来源、隐瞒依法或依规必须披露的 AI 使用情况，或绕过内容真实性检测。
- 不得承诺文件能够通过某种 AI 检测器；不得以规避检测为目标改写、篡改或处理文件。
- 对无法判断是否属于来源证明或必要披露的信息，先保留并标记待确认，不得直接删除。

### 2. 必须区分的两类标识

**A. 可清理的意外残留（在确认无必要后清理）**

例如：

- 未经要求而出现在正文或图中的“Generated by ChatGPT”“Created with [tool]”等工具宣传语或默认模板文字，但其并非必要的来源披露或授权要求。
- 提示词、对话语句、模型自述、内部任务说明、调试文字、临时文件名、占位符和未完成注释。
- 软件导出时自动加入、但与内容无关的默认标题、空白字段、无意义标签或模板残留。
- 不必要的个人隐私信息，例如本地用户名、临时目录、设备路径、GPS 位置或无关的个人元数据。
- 不属于最终交付内容的隐藏草稿、重复对象、废弃图层、隐藏幻灯片或无关批注。

**B. 不得擅自删除或篡改的来源与真实性标识**

包括但不限于：

- C2PA、Content Credentials、CAI 等内容来源与真实性凭证。
- SynthID 或其他用于内容来源识别、真实性验证的可见或不可见水印。
- 明确记录 AI 参与生成的来源元数据、审计记录或平台要求保留的披露信息。
- 版权声明、许可协议、作者署名、原始来源、第三方素材署名及必要的引用信息。
- 数字签名、文件完整性校验信息、机构或期刊要求的披露声明。
- 法律、期刊、资助机构、学校、客户或平台规则要求保留的 AI 使用说明。

不得为了让 AI 生成内容看起来像完全由人类创作、规避 AI 检测、绕过平台标识或隐瞒来源，而删除、覆盖、破坏或伪造上述信息。若用户要求删除此类标识，应说明原因，并提供保留来源信息的规范化排版、内容修订或合规披露方案。

### 3. 按文件类型检查

#### 3.1 Word、PowerPoint、Excel 等 Office 文件

检查：

- 文件名、标题、主题、作者、公司、管理者及自定义文档属性。
- 页眉、页脚、封面、脚注、尾注、图注、备注和演讲者备注。
- 批注、修订痕迹、隐藏文字、隐藏行列、隐藏工作表和隐藏幻灯片。
- 模板残留、默认占位符、未替换字段、内部提示词和调试说明。
- 图片替代文字、对象名称、嵌入文件、超链接及外部数据连接。

处理原则：

- 删除无关的临时内容和默认占位符。
- 清理不必要的个人隐私或临时路径信息。
- 保留真实作者信息、必要的署名、来源、引用、版权及合规披露；不得把作者字段改成未经证实的其他人。
- 检查文档在重新打开后排版、公式、图表、链接和可编辑性是否正常。

#### 3.2 图片、照片、插图和位图图表

检查：

- 图像中意外出现的工具界面、工具名称、提示词、错误文字、测试标签、默认角标和无关品牌标识。
- EXIF、IPTC、XMP 等元数据中的隐私信息、临时字段和不必要的默认软件信息。
- 隐藏缩略图、图层、蒙版、画布外对象和未使用的嵌入资源（如文件格式支持）。
- 图中文字、图例、比例尺、单位、符号、标签、边缘裁切和分辨率。

处理原则：

- 可以修正意外生成的乱码、重复文字、错误标签和非必要的工具宣传元素。
- 可以清理与交付无关的隐私元数据，但不得伪造拍摄设备、拍摄时间、作者或图像来源。
- 不得去除用于来源证明或真实性验证的水印、Content Credentials 或其他来源标识。
- 不得为了隐藏 AI 生成事实而重绘、裁切或覆盖来源水印。
- 修改后必须检查图像完整性、清晰度、色彩、比例、文字准确性及是否引入新的伪影。

#### 3.3 SVG、PDF 和其他矢量文件

检查：

- SVG/XML 注释、编辑器默认元数据、无关生成器字段、无用命名空间和临时对象 ID。
- SVG 的 `<title>`、`<desc>`、`<metadata>`、图层名称、隐藏对象、外部引用及嵌入图片。
- PDF 的 Info/XMP 元数据、批注、表单字段、隐藏图层、附件、书签和页面外对象。
- 字体嵌入、裁切框、画板尺寸、线条、箭头、文字转曲情况和矢量可编辑性。

处理原则：

- 仅清理无关的编辑器注释、临时标签和默认生成器字段；不得删除具有语义、可访问性或来源证明作用的内容。
- 保留必要的图层结构、文字说明、版权信息、来源凭证和数字签名。
- 不得通过删除元数据或重建 PDF 的方式破坏数字签名或内容凭证。
- 导出后重新打开并放大检查，确认文字、线条、箭头、符号和图层没有损坏。

#### 3.4 代码、脚本、配置文件和数据文件

检查：

- 文件头、注释、README、日志、包描述、作者字段和生成器字段。
- 临时路径、测试数据、调试输出、未使用变量、占位符、密钥或个人访问令牌。
- 自动生成代码的声明、许可证、版权声明、第三方代码来源和依赖信息。

处理原则：

- 删除无关的提示词、对话残留、调试输出和临时注释。
- 绝不将真实密钥、令牌或隐私信息留在交付文件中；发现时应按安全流程处理。
- 保留许可证、版权、第三方来源和依法或依规要求的生成声明。
- 不得删除必要的 generated-code 声明、依赖信息或来源记录，以造成代码由其他主体原创的错误印象。

#### 3.5 压缩包和多文件交付

- 检查压缩包内所有文件和目录名称，不只检查压缩包本身。
- 删除缓存、临时文件、重复文件、无关日志和系统垃圾文件。
- 检查是否包含错误版本、未完成草稿、私人信息或意外嵌入的提示词。
- 保留必要的 README、许可证、引用、来源记录和复现文件。
- 解压测试，确认目录结构完整、文件可打开、文件数量与交付清单一致。

### 4. 内容层面的检查

- 删除不属于最终内容的模型自述、对话语气、任务指令、提示词和“以下是我为你生成的”等交互性套话。
- 将不符合正式文体的表达改为准确、自然、专业的语言；不得仅为改变 AI 检测结果而进行无依据的同义词替换。
- 检查是否存在虚构的作者、机构、实验、数据、文献、DOI、软件版本、参数或结果。
- 所有事实、数字、引用、图表标签和实验描述必须与原始资料及实际分析记录一致。
- 对 AI 生成或辅助生成的科学图表，逐项核对基因名、物种名、结构、通路方向、单位、比例尺、统计符号和数据映射。
- 对无法验证的内容明确标注待确认，不得以润色掩盖不确定性。

### 5. 最终交付检查流程

1. **文件检查：** 检查文件名、目录、格式、大小、可打开性及是否存在损坏。
2. **内容检查：** 检查正文、图像、图注、表格、备注、隐藏内容和嵌入资源。
3. **元数据检查：** 检查文档属性、EXIF/IPTC/XMP、SVG/XML、PDF Info/XMP 和代码头信息。
4. **标识分类：** 区分意外残留与来源、版权、真实性或合规披露标识。
5. **清理与修复：** 只处理确认可清理的无关内容，不破坏必要标识。
6. **重新打开验证：** 使用目标软件重新打开文件，检查布局、图层、字体、图表、链接、公式和可编辑性。
7. **交付复核：** 确认文件内容与用户要求一致，并保留必要的来源和版本记录。

### 6. 交付前检查清单

- [ ] 文件名和目录结构整洁、明确。
- [ ] 正文和图像中没有意外残留的提示词、对话内容、调试信息或占位符。
- [ ] 没有无关的工具宣传文字、默认角标或模板残留。
- [ ] 文档属性和元数据中没有不必要的隐私信息或临时路径。
- [ ] 已检查隐藏文字、批注、修订痕迹、图层、备注和嵌入对象。
- [ ] 图片、SVG、PDF 和 Office 文件均已重新打开检查。
- [ ] 真实作者、版权、许可证、引用、来源及必要的 AI 披露信息得到保留。
- [ ] 未删除、篡改或伪造 C2PA、Content Credentials、SynthID、数字签名或其他真实性凭证。
- [ ] 没有为了规避 AI 检测或隐瞒来源而处理文件。
- [ ] 数据、图表、文字和分析结果均已核实，没有虚构内容。
- [ ] 最终文件可正常打开、显示、编辑和复现。

**核心原则：清理意外残留，不伪造来源；优化交付质量，不隐瞒真实性。**

## English Version

### 1. General Requirements

- Inspect both the file itself and its contents before every formal delivery. Checking only the visible pages is insufficient.
- The inspection scope includes filenames, visible text, images, headers, footers, captions, notes, comments, hidden objects, layers, document properties, metadata, embedded resources, and source-code comments.
- Remove accidental tool prompts, conversation remnants, temporary labels, debugging information, default placeholders, irrelevant promotional branding, and generation-process notes that do not belong in the final deliverable.
- Do not interpret cleanup as permission to fabricate authorship or provenance, conceal AI use when disclosure is legally or institutionally required, or bypass content-authenticity checks.
- Do not promise that a file will pass a particular AI detector. Do not edit, manipulate, or process files for the purpose of evading detection.
- If it is unclear whether an item is a provenance record or required disclosure, retain it and flag it for confirmation rather than deleting it.

### 2. Distinguish Two Types of Identifiers

**A. Accidental artifacts that may be cleaned after verification**

Examples include:

- Unrequested tool slogans or default template text such as “Generated by ChatGPT” or “Created with [tool]”, provided they are not required provenance disclosures or licensing notices.
- Prompts, conversational sentences, model self-references, internal task instructions, debugging text, temporary filenames, placeholders, and unfinished comments.
- Default titles, empty fields, meaningless labels, or template remnants inserted by export software that are unrelated to the final content.
- Unnecessary personal information, such as local usernames, temporary directories, device paths, GPS coordinates, or irrelevant personal metadata.
- Hidden drafts, duplicate objects, obsolete layers, hidden slides, or irrelevant comments that are not part of the final deliverable.

**B. Provenance and authenticity identifiers that must not be removed or altered without authorization**

These include, but are not limited to:

- C2PA, Content Credentials, CAI, and other content provenance or authenticity credentials.
- SynthID or other visible or invisible watermarks used for provenance identification or authenticity verification.
- Provenance metadata, audit records, or disclosure information that explicitly records AI involvement or is required by the platform.
- Copyright notices, license terms, author attribution, original sources, third-party asset credits, and required citations.
- Digital signatures, file-integrity verification information, and disclosure statements required by an institution or journal.
- AI-use statements required by law, journals, funders, universities, clients, or platform policies.

Do not delete, overwrite, damage, or falsify these records to make AI-generated content appear entirely human-created, evade AI detection, bypass platform labels, or conceal its origin. If asked to remove such identifiers, explain why they must be preserved and offer compliant formatting, content revision, or disclosure alternatives.

### 3. File-type-specific Inspection

#### 3.1 Word, PowerPoint, Excel, and Other Office Files

Inspect:

- Filename, title, subject, author, company, manager, and custom document properties.
- Headers, footers, title pages, footnotes, endnotes, captions, comments, and speaker notes.
- Comments, tracked changes, hidden text, hidden rows or columns, hidden worksheets, and hidden slides.
- Template remnants, default placeholders, unreplaced fields, internal prompts, and debugging notes.
- Image alternative text, object names, embedded files, hyperlinks, and external data connections.

Handling rules:

- Remove irrelevant temporary content and default placeholders.
- Clean unnecessary personal information and temporary path data.
- Preserve genuine authorship, required attribution, sources, citations, copyright, and compliance disclosures. Never replace an author field with an unverified person.
- Reopen the document and verify that formatting, formulas, charts, links, and editability remain intact.

#### 3.2 Images, Photographs, Illustrations, and Raster Charts

Inspect:

- Accidental tool interfaces, tool names, prompts, erroneous text, test labels, default badges, and irrelevant branding visible in the image.
- Privacy information, temporary fields, and unnecessary default software information in EXIF, IPTC, and XMP metadata.
- Hidden thumbnails, layers, masks, off-canvas objects, and unused embedded resources, where supported by the format.
- Text, legends, scale bars, units, symbols, labels, edge cropping, and resolution.

Handling rules:

- Correct accidental gibberish, duplicated text, incorrect labels, and nonessential promotional elements.
- Remove privacy metadata unrelated to delivery, but never falsify the capture device, capture time, author, or image source.
- Do not remove provenance or authenticity watermarks, Content Credentials, or other source identifiers.
- Do not redraw, crop, or cover provenance watermarks to conceal AI generation.
- After editing, inspect image integrity, sharpness, colors, proportions, text accuracy, and newly introduced artifacts.

#### 3.3 SVG, PDF, and Other Vector Files

Inspect:

- SVG/XML comments, editor-default metadata, irrelevant generator fields, unnecessary namespaces, and temporary object IDs.
- SVG `<title>`, `<desc>`, `<metadata>`, layer names, hidden objects, external references, and embedded images.
- PDF Info/XMP metadata, annotations, form fields, hidden layers, attachments, bookmarks, and off-page objects.
- Font embedding, clipping boxes, canvas dimensions, strokes, arrows, text outlining, and vector editability.

Handling rules:

- Remove only irrelevant editor comments, temporary labels, and default generator fields. Do not remove semantic, accessibility, or provenance information.
- Preserve necessary layer structures, descriptions, copyright information, provenance credentials, and digital signatures.
- Do not damage digital signatures or content credentials by deleting metadata or rebuilding a PDF.
- Reopen exported files and inspect them at high magnification to ensure that text, lines, arrows, symbols, and layers remain intact.

#### 3.4 Code, Scripts, Configuration Files, and Data Files

Inspect:

- File headers, comments, README files, logs, package descriptions, author fields, and generator fields.
- Temporary paths, test data, debugging output, unused variables, placeholders, secrets, and personal access tokens.
- Generated-code declarations, licenses, copyright notices, third-party code sources, and dependency information.

Handling rules:

- Remove irrelevant prompts, conversation remnants, debugging output, and temporary comments.
- Never leave genuine secrets, tokens, or private information in deliverables. If discovered, handle them according to the applicable security procedure.
- Preserve licenses, copyright notices, third-party attribution, and generation declarations required by law or policy.
- Do not remove necessary generated-code declarations, dependency information, or provenance records in a way that falsely implies original authorship by another party.

#### 3.5 Archives and Multi-file Deliverables

- Inspect every filename and directory inside an archive, not only the archive filename.
- Remove caches, temporary files, duplicates, irrelevant logs, and operating-system clutter.
- Check for incorrect versions, unfinished drafts, private information, and accidentally embedded prompts.
- Preserve necessary README files, licenses, citations, provenance records, and reproducibility materials.
- Test extraction and confirm that the directory structure is complete, files open correctly, and the file count matches the delivery manifest.

### 4. Content-level Inspection

- Remove model self-references, conversational language, task instructions, prompts, and phrases such as “Here is what I generated for you” when they do not belong in the final content.
- Revise informal or unsuitable wording into accurate, natural, professional language. Do not perform unsupported synonym substitution merely to alter AI-detector outcomes.
- Check for fabricated authors, institutions, experiments, data, references, DOIs, software versions, parameters, or results.
- Ensure that all facts, numbers, citations, figure labels, and experimental descriptions agree with source materials and actual analysis records.
- For AI-generated or AI-assisted scientific figures, verify gene names, species names, structures, pathway directions, units, scale bars, statistical symbols, and data mappings individually.
- Explicitly flag unverifiable content for confirmation. Do not use polishing to conceal uncertainty.

### 5. Final Delivery Workflow

1. **File inspection:** Check filenames, directories, formats, file sizes, openability, and possible corruption.
2. **Content inspection:** Check text, images, captions, tables, notes, hidden content, and embedded resources.
3. **Metadata inspection:** Check document properties, EXIF/IPTC/XMP, SVG/XML, PDF Info/XMP, and source-code headers.
4. **Identifier classification:** Distinguish accidental artifacts from provenance, copyright, authenticity, or compliance-disclosure identifiers.
5. **Cleanup and repair:** Process only confirmed irrelevant artifacts; do not damage required identifiers.
6. **Reopen and verify:** Reopen files in their target applications and check layout, layers, fonts, charts, links, formulas, and editability.
7. **Delivery review:** Confirm that the files meet the user's requirements and retain necessary provenance and version records.

### 6. Pre-delivery Checklist

- [ ] Filenames and directory structure are clean and unambiguous.
- [ ] No accidental prompts, conversational remnants, debugging text, or placeholders remain in the text or images.
- [ ] No irrelevant tool promotions, default badges, or template remnants remain.
- [ ] Document properties and metadata contain no unnecessary private information or temporary paths.
- [ ] Hidden text, comments, tracked changes, layers, notes, and embedded objects have been inspected.
- [ ] Images, SVG, PDF, and Office files have been reopened and checked.
- [ ] Genuine authorship, copyright, licenses, citations, provenance, and required AI disclosures are preserved.
- [ ] C2PA, Content Credentials, SynthID, digital signatures, and other authenticity credentials have not been removed, altered, or falsified.
- [ ] No processing has been performed to evade AI detection or conceal provenance.
- [ ] Data, figures, text, and analytical results have been verified and contain no fabricated information.
- [ ] Final files open, display, edit, and reproduce correctly.

**Core principle: remove accidental artifacts, never falsify provenance; improve delivery quality, never conceal authenticity.**
