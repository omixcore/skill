# Gene Family Analysis — Figure Examples

This gallery organizes the PNG examples contributed to the Gene Family Analysis Skill. Images are grouped by analytical module and use descriptive, lowercase kebab-case filenames.

These files are visual references for figure structure and analysis outputs. They are not validated datasets, universal templates, or evidence that the underlying biological conclusions are correct.

## Directory structure

- `01-phylogenetic-analysis/` — phylogenetic trees and subgroup annotations
- `02-domain-and-motif-analysis/` — conserved domains and motif architectures
- `03-promoter-analysis/` — cis-regulatory element composition and distribution
- `04-inter-species-synteny/` — comparative synteny across species
- `05-chromosome-and-intra-species-synteny/` — chromosome localization and duplication links
- `06-ka-ks-analysis/` — Ka, Ks, and Ka/Ks comparisons
- `08-protein-protein-interaction/` — protein-association networks
- `09-expression-analysis/` — gene-family expression heatmaps
- `combined-figures/` — figures spanning multiple analytical modules
- `99-needs-review/` — files awaiting visual identification

## Review notes

- Four images could not be confidently identified during visual inspection. They are retained in `99-needs-review/` and have not been assigned speculative scientific labels.
- The chromosome Circos example contains a visible third-party watermark. Verify provenance, permission, and licensing before redistribution, publication, or reuse beyond internal reference.
- The two multispecies circular-tree examples are related in layout but were retained as separate files; no exact duplicate was identified in the inspected set.
- Composite figures are stored once under `combined-figures/` and cross-referenced from the relevant modules to avoid duplicate copies.
- No uploaded image was deleted or altered. This reorganization changes paths and filenames only.
