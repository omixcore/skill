# Research Skills

A curated collection of reusable standards and workflows for scientific writing, visualization, analysis, software development, and research operations.

## Writing

- [De-AI Writing Skill](writing/de-ai-writing/SKILL.md) — diagnose and improve formulaic, stiff, generic, or translation-like Chinese and English writing while preserving author voice and factual integrity.
- [AI-assisted Academic Editing](writing/ai-assisted-academic-editing.md) — academic language editing, authorial voice, scientific fidelity, and responsible AI-assisted writing.
- [Reference Verification](writing/reference-verification.md) — verify reference authenticity and whether each source supports the specific claim.
- [Author Information](writing/author-information.md) — author names, affiliations, and contributor information.
- [Funding and Conflict Statements](writing/funding-conflict-statements.md) — funding and competing-interest statements.

## Repository principles

- [AI-assisted Artifact Hygiene](principles/ai-assisted-artifact-hygiene.md) — inspect and prepare reliable, clean, traceable research deliverables.
- [Independent Reasoning and Verification](principles/independent-reasoning-and-verification.md) — maintain independent reasoning and verify important claims and outputs.
- [Project Version Standard](development/project-version-standard.md) — use the repository's unified project version convention.
